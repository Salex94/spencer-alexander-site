Referring a client to us

How to refer a client, what we do for the people you send us, how their fees are set and how we keep you informed.

Accountants, financial planners and other advisers send us clients when a family, an estate or a business needs a lawyer. We reply to every referral within one business day, we quote defined work at the published prices on our fees page, and with your client's consent we can keep you informed as the matter moves.

How do I refer a client?

The simplest way is to give your client our number, (03) 9125 8355, and ask them to mention your name when they call. If your client would rather we called them, and they have agreed to you passing on their details, email contact@spenceralexander.com.au with their name, the best number to reach them and a line about what they need. Your client's first call is free, and they speak with a lawyer.

What work do we take on?

We act for individuals, families and businesses in Family Law, Wills and Estates and Commercial Law, which covers most of what an adviser sees across a client's life. That includes a will, an enduring power of attorney or a testamentary trust, a probate application or a dispute over an estate, a separation and the property settlement that follows it, and for a business, its structure, contracts, leases, sale, shareholder agreement and unpaid debts. Our Family Law, Wills and Estates and Commercial Law pages describe each area in more detail.

What will it cost your client?

Defined work, such as a will, a probate application for a straightforward estate, a divorce application or setting up a company, has a fixed fee on our fees page, shown as the total including GST. Anything else is estimated in writing in an engagement letter and costs agreement before substantive work begins, so your client decides with the numbers in front of them.

Will you keep me informed?

Yes, with your client's consent. What a client tells us is confidential, so we report back to you only when they agree. Where they do, we can work alongside you so that the legal and the financial advice point the same way, which matters most where an estate plan has to fit a family trust, a separation reaches into a business or a sale has tax consequences.

Who looks after the work?

Every matter is overseen by the principal, Spencer Alexander, who has more than ten years of legal experience, was admitted as a lawyer in 2018 and is a member of the Law Institute of Victoria. You can read more about Spencer and the firm.

To talk about a referral, or about how we might work with your clients, call (03) 9125 8355 or email contact@spenceralexander.com.au.
