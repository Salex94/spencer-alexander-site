Time limits

Some matters cannot wait.

Family Law

Separation, safety and settlement

A property settlement claim must generally be made within 12 months of a divorce order taking effect, or within two years of a de facto separation. If safety is at risk, call 000 first, then us.

Wills & Estates

Contesting a will

A family provision claim in Victoria must generally be started within six months of the grant of probate or administration, and a late claim needs the court's permission.

Commercial Law

A statutory demand or letter of demand

A company served with a statutory demand has 21 days to respond before it is presumed insolvent, so send it, or any court document, to us the day it arrives.
