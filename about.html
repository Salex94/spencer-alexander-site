Meet Spencer Alexander.

Spencer Alexander
Principal

Spencer Alexander is a Melbourne lawyer who believes good advice begins with listening. For more than a decade he has sat across the table from people on some of the hardest days of their lives, and what they remember afterwards is not just the law, but the feeling of being genuinely heard, looked after, and fought for.

Over more than ten years he has practised across Commercial Law, Family Law and Wills and Estates, internationally and in the Australian private sector, and that breadth shows in advice that is commercially grounded, legally rigorous and delivered in plain language.

He started Spencer Alexander Lawyers because he had seen too many people passed between junior staff and left in the dark about their own matter. Here you deal with senior lawyers directly, from the first phone call to the last, and your matter is given the seniority, discretion and care it deserves.
