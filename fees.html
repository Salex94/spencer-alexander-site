What the prices do not include

Court and government filing fees are set by government and usually change on 1 July. For a divorce or consent orders, you pay the Federal Circuit and Family Court's filing fee directly to the Court at the rate set by regulation. For a divorce, a reduced fee applies to people who qualify, including holders of a health care card, pensioner concession card or Commonwealth seniors health card, people with legal aid and people the Court accepts are in financial hardship. For consent orders, people in those groups pay no fee. In a joint application, each of you must qualify, or the full fee applies. For a grant of probate or letters of administration, the Supreme Court's filing fee depends on the gross value of the estate's assets in Victoria and is nil where that value is less than $250,000. ASIC's fee for registering a company is passed on at cost, and the fee for publishing the notice of intention to apply for a grant is included in our probate and administration fees. Third party costs that depend on the matter, such as process servers, valuations and searches, are paid at cost, and your costs agreement sets out each of them before work begins.

When a fixed fee does not fit

A dispute, anything outside the work described, or a matter where one of the assumptions above does not hold is charged on a written estimate instead, set out in a costs agreement before any substantive work begins. No piece of work costs less than $495 including GST, which is an hour of a lawyer's time. The first call is where we work out which applies to you.
