98Who can contest my will, and can I reduce the risk?谁可以对我的遗嘱提出异议？我能降低这种风险吗？
99In Victoria, certain people, typically a spouse or partner, children and some dependants, can ask the court for a share, or a larger share, of an estate if they were left without adequate provision. The risk can never be removed entirely, but careful drafting, and a clear record of your reasons, reduces it considerably. We advise you where your will is exposed and what to do about it.在维多利亚州，某些人，通常是配偶或伴侣、子女以及部分受扶养人，如果没有从遗产中得到足够的供养，可以请求法院判给他们一份遗产，或更大的份额。这种风险永远无法完全消除，但审慎起草遗嘱，并清楚记录您的理由，可以大大降低风险。我们会告诉您遗嘱在哪些方面容易受到质疑，以及应该怎样处理。"left without adequate provision" is 没有从遗产中得到足够的供养, the same wording as the Part IV paragraph above.
100Does my superannuation follow my will?我的养老金会按遗嘱分配吗？
101Not automatically. Superannuation is paid by your fund's trustee, and unless you have a valid, current binding death benefit nomination, the trustee decides who receives it. For many people super is one of their largest assets, so we make sure your nomination and your will work together rather than against each other.不会自动按遗嘱分配。养老金由您的基金受托人支付；除非您有一份有效、未过期且具约束力的身故受益人提名，否则由受托人决定谁获得这笔钱。对许多人来说，养老金是他们最大的资产之一，所以我们会确保您的提名与遗嘱互相配合，而不是互相抵触。"valid, current" is 有效且未过期, which covers a nomination that has lapsed.
102What is the difference between a power of attorney and a medical treatment decision maker?授权书与医疗决策人有什么区别？
103An enduring power of attorney lets someone you trust make financial and personal decisions if you cannot. An appointed medical treatment decision maker makes medical treatment decisions in the same circumstances. Both operate while you are alive; your will only speaks after death. The three documents together are what make an estate plan complete.持久授权书让您信任的人在您无法自行决定时，代您作出财务和个人事务方面的决定。您委任的医疗决策人则在同样情况下代您作出医疗决定。两者都在您在世时发挥作用；遗嘱只在您去世后才发挥作用。三份文件结合起来，遗产规划才算完整。

105An executor locates the will, secures the assets, applies for probate where needed, pays the estate's debts and distributes what remains, and is personally responsible for doing it properly. 108Can I just use a will kit?可以直接用自助遗嘱套件吗？
