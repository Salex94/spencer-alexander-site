Enquiry acknowledgement email: translation check

When someone sends an enquiry through the website form, they now receive an automatic email confirming it arrived. This is the Chinese version, which will be sent to enquiries from the Chinese contact page once you have checked it and the Chinese pages are live. Until then, everyone receives the English version.

Please check each Chinese line against the English beside it, in Suggesting mode, or add a comment where you are unsure. The same rules apply as for the pages: no dashes of any kind, no brackets apart from the phone number, no 专家, and nothing that suggests a lawyer at the firm speaks Mandarin. Two lines need particular care. The documents line tells the enquirer that the firm is not acting for them until both sides agree it will. The unsubscribe line is required by the Spam Act 2003 and must say that emailing contact@spenceralexander.com.au with unsubscribe in the subject stops further emails other than about their enquiry. The sign off is 谨致问候 followed by Spencer; please say if another sign off reads better. The email is signed with Spencer’s usual English signature, which stays in English.
