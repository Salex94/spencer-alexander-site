# Fixed fees at four out of ten, 16 November 2026

Spencer's instruction of 16 November 2026: every fixed fee is four out of ten, which he defined as just below the average, the average published price of at least ten to twenty Victorian firms, or as many as publish one, less 10 per cent. Work that is not on the fees page is assessed the same way. This record holds the survey behind the prices published on 16 November 2026.

## Method

Every price was read on 16 November 2026, either on the firm's own page or on the search engine's current copy of that page, and each is recorded in prices.csv with the wording as published, the link and the date. A firm counts when it is a law practice with an office in Victoria. National online firms with no Victorian office shown, trustee companies, online will and document services, referral services and guides were left out, and so were pages that showed no figure, prices dated 2023 or earlier, and services whose scope could not be matched. excluded.csv lists each one and the reason.

Each price was brought to the firm's own scope and footing: the total including GST, with GST added where a price was stated before GST, and a price whose GST treatment is not stated treated as including GST, which errs low. A from price was taken at its from figure and a range at its lowest figure. Court and government fees were removed. For probate and letters of administration the firm's fee includes publishing the notice of intention to apply, so the Supreme Court's $38 fee for 2026 to 2027 was added to each price that leaves it out. ASIC's $636 registration fee was taken out of company set up prices that include it, and where a firm's document price excludes a compulsory first meeting, the meeting was added.

The four out of ten figure is 90 per cent of the plain average. On Spencer's instruction later the same morning, each published total including GST is that figure rounded down to the nearest amount five dollars under a hundred, so a single will at four out of ten of $523.72 is $495 and no fee sits above four out of ten. The amount before GST shown beneath it is the total divided by 1.1, to the cent. No listed fee is under $495 or over $5,000 including GST.

## Where the market is thin

Spencer allowed fewer than ten firms where no more publish a price. Sixteen services have fewer than ten, and seven have four or fewer: wills with testamentary trusts for a couple, consent orders for property and parenting together, company set up and the letter of demand with four each, terms of trade with three, and the parenting plan and the business purchase review with two each. Most Victorian commercial firms quote privately, and the 9 November 2026 survey found only seven of 866 Victorian commercial firm websites publishing a usable price, so the published commercial average leans on a few online firms and is probably below what the whole market charges.
