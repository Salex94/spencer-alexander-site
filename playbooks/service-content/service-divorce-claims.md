## Generalised or not verified

- "Domiciled, which broadly means living here with the intention of staying permanently" is a plain summary of domicile, not the full test, which also covers a domicile of origin a person has not replaced.
- "Children of the marriage" follows ss 55A(3) and 98A(3), which include children treated as members of the family; the page does not spell out that definition.
- "By hand from anyone but you" summarises rules 2.35 and 2.36; service through a lawyer who agrees to accept it also counts as personal service.
- The evidence needed to prove separation under one roof is described only as "the evidence the Court needs". The Court's forms and practice on affidavits could not be read because the court site is blocked.
- Whether an overseas marriage certificate must be filed is a matter of the Court's forms, not the Rules, so the page states the translation rule only for documents filed and does not say the certificate must be filed.
- The voice checker fails the Sources line for the brackets in the official titles, (Cth), (Vic), (Family Law) and (Fees). The brief allows (Cth) and (Vic), the other two are part of the instruments' official names, and the articles' Sources lines already cite titles such as Supreme Court (Fees) Regulations 2018 (Vic) the same way. Every other line of visible text passes the checker with no failures and no notes.
- The 28 day response time is stated as "generally" because the Court's own information on service overseas could not be read.
- No court fee figure, no processing time and no statement about how long a divorce takes appear on the page.
- Rule 36 of the Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules was not read.

## Possible errors noticed elsewhere, not changed

- family-law.html In brief: "within 12 months of a divorce order" should run from the order taking effect, s 44(3)(c), and omits the consent or leave rule.
- family-law.html FAQ "Is there a time limit on property settlement?": "Outside those limits you need the court's permission" omits both parties' consent, s 44(3) and (5)(b).
- faq.html "Is there a time limit for a property settlement after separation?": same omission of consent.
- fees.html "What the prices do not include": the Court's fee is set by regulation, not by the Court, and for consent orders card holders are exempt and hardship leads to a waiver, not a reduced fee, Fees Regulations ss 2.04, 2.05 and 2.06. For a divorce the reduced fee statement is correct.
- resource-separation-first-30-days.html and insight-property-after-separation.html, outside the requested scope: the first omits consent, and the second says "within twelve months of divorce" rather than of the divorce order taking effect.
