## Generalised or left out

- The small business threshold figure is not stated. The Act sets $200,000 or a prescribed amount up to $500,000; the regulation was not read today, so the page states the test, not the figure, and the matching article carries the figure.
- The s 52 repayment consequence, s 52(4), was cut for length, not for doubt.
- The tenant's rights in RLA s 17(2) to (6), a notice between 7 and 90 days after entering the lease and termination within 28 days, are not stated; the page states only s 17(1) and (1C).
- Enterprise agreements transferring under FWA s 313 were cut for length; the page does not rely on them.
- Non retail leases: the page says nothing about them beyond limiting each lease statement to a retail lease.
- Practice statements, not law: "people usually call at one of three points" and "transferring the lease usually needs the landlord's consent".
- What to bring: the listing or information memorandum, any offer, the draft contract and the section 52 statement if the buyer has one; for a seller, the lease and latest financial statements; and questions. Each is a document the person plainly holds; no legal requirement to hold or bring anything is stated, and the line that little on paper is fine is kept.
