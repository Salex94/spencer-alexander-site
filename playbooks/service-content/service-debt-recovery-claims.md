## Generalised or left out, and why

- Enforcement methods, the warrant to seize property, attachment of earnings and debts, oral examination and charging land, are left to the article; the page says only that we advise on enforcing a judgment.
- Interest on judgment debts, MCA s 100(7), and the Penalty Interest Rates Act are not stated.
- Retention of title and PPSR timing, the audit's A2 finding, are left to the contracts, terms and debt articles.
- No court fee or scale cost figure is stated.
- Practice statements, not law: when businesses call, that acting early usually keeps more options open, that a letter of demand often resolves an undisputed debt, that most unpaid invoices fall within the Magistrates' Court limit, that a court claim is usually the better course for a disputed debt, and that we weigh cost against the prospect of recovery, which is the hub's wording.
- What to have with you names invoices, statements of account, terms of trade or the contract, any guarantee, correspondence and any statutory demand with its affidavit, all documents a creditor or a served company plainly holds; it states no legal requirement and keeps the line that having little on paper is fine.
