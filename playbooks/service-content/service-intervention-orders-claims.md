in Melbourne, on Spencer's instruction of that day to implement every change the review had recommended, one of which

Read today on legislation.vic.gov.au, in the Word copy of the authorised version:

- Family Violence Protection Act 2008 (Vic), version 071, incorporating amendments as at 10 November 2026: ss 5, 8,
  24, 31, 45, 45A, 53, 54, 57, 60, 60G, 74, 76A, 78, 81, 95, 96, 96B, 96C, 99, 108, 109, 123, 125, 146 and 242.
- Personal Safety Intervention Orders Act 2010 (Vic), version 037, incorporating amendments as at 10 November 2026:
  ss 1 and 61.

Every statement of law on the page is one the site's intervention orders article already makes, or follows the words
of a section read today. The page quotes no fee, because intervention order work is charged on a written estimate.
