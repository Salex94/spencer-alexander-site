## What it means

The fees are not too cheap across the board when set against published law firm prices. Two items sit well below the 3.5 to 4 out of 10 target: wills for a couple at P22 and probate at P17. The single will and letters of administration are already inside the target band. Both estate plans sit at the median, a little above target, so the market case is to bring them down slightly, not up. The stand alone attorney documents sit at the top of the market only because of the $495 minimum.

They are all too cheap for Spencer's time. At $450 plus GST an hour, not one item at the recommended price pays for the hours a sole principal is likely to spend doing every step himself. The single will pays for one hour against about two and a half. The estate plan for one person pays for two hours against about four. Probate is the only item where a price that pays his full rate, about $2,723 for five and a half hours, still sits in the middle of the market, at P59. At these prices a will can only pay through cheaper hours, such as a paralegal's, through document automation and online intake, or by being the first matter in a relationship that later brings the probate and the administration. Spencer has three levers, and they work best together: cutting the time per will to between one and two hours, pricing above the 3.5 mark where the work cannot be cut, which the market allows for probate, and treating wills and estate plans as farming that the estate work repays years later. The time test below sets out the numbers.

The sample leans cheap. Firms that publish prices tend to be the ones competing on price. ## Method

Searching covered fixed fee wills, estate plans, attorney documents, testamentary trust wills, probate and letters of administration across the eastern suburbs, the rest of Melbourne, regional Victoria, national online law firms, trustee companies and online platforms. The session's shared web search allowance ran out part way through, so the last rounds fetched provider pages already identified rather than searching further. That cost the most in testamentary trusts, probate and letters of administration, where the published market is also thinnest.

Each price was recorded with the provider, place, type, item, the price exactly as published, its GST treatment, what it covers and excludes, whether disbursements are included, the URL and the date read. prices.csv holds 118 rows from 34 providers: 26 law firms, one trustee company, State Trustees, and seven online platforms or do it yourself services, with State Trustees' own online will counted among the platforms. excluded.csv lists what was left out and why.

Normalisation, to the legal fee including GST for the firm's scope:

- A price stated before GST had 10 per cent added: Nevett Ford, Lorraine Jones, dlegal, Eve Legal and Galbally & O'Bryan.
- A price whose GST treatment is not stated was kept, treated as including GST and flagged: Clearwill, Van Beveren, Notary Public Geelong, Maitland Legal, McManus & Co, Lord Law and Alderuccio. If any of these are really before GST, the market is dearer than shown, so the treatment errs low.
- Court filing fees were removed. For probate and administration, the firm's price includes publishing the notice of intention to apply, which costs $38.00 from 1 July 2026 on the Supreme Court's fee list. That $38 was added to every price that excludes the notice or does not say. Victoria Probate's package includes a $55 notice fee and $36.20 express post, so its legal fee of $1,650 including GST had the $38 notice added instead.
- Eve Legal prices probate in two parts, a fixed $250 initial conference and a fixed $2,750 for up to two assets, so both were added before GST was applied.
- A "from" price was taken at its from figure. dlegal's range of $330 to $440 was taken at $330. Papson Legal's per person plan was doubled for a couple.
- Percentiles use linear interpolation between closest ranks, the same as PERCENTILE.INC in Excel and the default in numpy. The firm's position uses the same interpolation in reverse.
- The recommendation keeps the current price when it already sits between P35 and P40. Otherwise it takes a clean amount before GST, a multiple of $50 or an amount ending in 90 in the style of $990, whose total sits inside the band, preferring one that also sits inside the band when stale pages are removed and when only prices that state GST are counted. Nothing goes below $495.
- Two checks run beside every item: one removing four pages that look stale, which are Notary Public Geelong dated 2022, McManus & Co and Buller McLeod dated 2023, and Papson Legal, whose files date from 2014; and one keeping only prices that state their GST treatment.

Spencer's own scale, average at 5 and the dearest at 10 with 3.5 as the target, gives 70 per cent of the mean when the dearest price is twice the average, so 70 and 80 per cent of the law firm mean appear for each item as the cross check. For wills, plans and attorney documents the dearest firm charges only 1.4 to 1.8 times the average, so prices sit closer together than in his example, and 70 to 80 per cent of the mean reaches further into the cheap end: it lands between P9 and P33. For probate and administration the dearest is about 2.1 times the average, as in his example, and the cross check lands between P16 and P42, close to the band. Where the two disagree, the percentile band is the better reading of 3.5 to 4 out of 10, because it counts firms rather than dollars and one dear outlier cannot move it.

## Market patterns worth knowing

What firms near the recommended level include. The firms near $990 for one person's plan are RV Legal at $1,000, dlegal at $1,056, Eve Legal at $1,089, Silk Legal and Van Beveren at $880 and State Trustees at $990. Clearwill includes certified copies, Maitland Legal allows unlimited changes for six months, Silk Legal discounts for pensioners, seniors and health care card holders, and State Trustees takes 20 per cent off for concession card holders and for naming it as executor. The firm's fees page already names the advice meeting, one round of changes and the signing appointment, which is as clear as any. It says nothing about keeping the originals; if the firm offers safe custody, saying so would match what most firms at this level list.

Per couple or per person. Wills for a couple are sold as a package at a median 1.72 times the single will across ten firms, from 1.33 to 2.00 times, and couple plans at a median 1.82 times across fifteen. Attorney documents are mostly priced per person, and Nevett Ford, Pearsons, Lorraine Jones and Alderuccio show a couple's pair at about 1.7 to 2 times one. With the single will held at $495, wills for a couple at $990 are exactly two single wills, so the fees page would show no saving for couples, unlike most firms. Names. Presentation that sells. Pearsons prints the dollar saving beside each package, "Save $274.50 versus booking separately", and State Trustees prints "save $130" and "save $300". Probate conventions. One price for probate and administration is the norm: all seven law firms with an administration price charge the same as for probate.
