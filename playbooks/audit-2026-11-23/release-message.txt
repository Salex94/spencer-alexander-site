Audit round of 23 Nov 2026: six fees restored from one fee source, and the improvements the audit supports

Released as one commit so that it can be undone in one step with git revert.
The record is playbooks/audit-2026-11-23, whose README gives the release
steps, and changes.md there lists every change a visitor can see.

Fees. The six fees that rose on 16 Nov 2026 are back at the amounts published
from 9 to 16 Nov 2026: probate application $1,980, letters of administration
$2,178, wills for a couple $880, consent orders for an agreed property
settlement $3,025, consent orders for property and parenting together $3,850
and advice on a binding financial agreement the other party drafted from
$1,100. Each restored row is byte for byte the row deployed that week, and the
other eighteen fees are unchanged.

One fee source. playbooks/fees.json holds each fee once and scripts/fees.py
keeps every rendition in step with it. playbooks/fees-renditions.json records
every sentence that quotes a fee or a charge with each figure tied to its fee,
the paragraph it stands in and the heading or question above it, and the gate
fails a sentence that is new, changed or gone, or whose paragraph, heading or
question has changed, until it has been read and recorded. Every passage that
quotes the firm's fees says whose they are, where, in what currency, as at
what day, who published them and where.

Court and ASIC charges, read on 23 Nov 2026, are published on the fees page
and beside the fees they add to, each dated and sourced, with the last day
they are current.

Pages. Every price at a glance and dated on the fees page, a fees line in the
home hero, the reply time on every rail card, safe contact wording and
emergency numbers that can be tapped on the family pages, the Quick exit on
the contact page, Sources and closing lines on the three hubs, readable
service text on phones, a narrower measure on desktops, a caption track on
the film that a visitor switches on, passages that name their Act, court or
State, a line under each service page's and each hub's summary saying who
published it, when, and the legislation it rests on, and a fee note that reads
at the size and measure of the text around it.

Faults fixed. The menu button and footer wordmark cut off at 320 pixels, an
enquiry sent twice on a slow connection, keyboard focus hidden under the
header or on a block yet to fade in, the form's error not tied to its fields,
the contrast of the word Lawyers under the wordmark, and a call button whose
label hung outside it on the narrowest phones.

Claims register. One statement of law was corrected: the powers of attorney
page now says the regulations prescribe the form, from section 32 of the
Powers of Attorney Act 2014 (Vic) and regulation 6(1) of the Powers of
Attorney Regulations 2025 (Vic). The three hubs gained Sources lines, each
provision read in its authorised version on 23 Nov 2026. Sentences that
gained the name of an Act, a court or a State were each read against that
Act the same day, apart from the seven urgent strips and the divorce
answers, whose names were taken from the pages' claims registers. No other
statement of law changed.

Routines. A practice hub's closing line gives the day it was updated and the
month its law was read as two statements, so the Monday article routine dates
a hub with scripts/fees.py stamp after adding its link and the month stays
where it is. playbooks/article.md, site-health.md and demand-scanner.md say
what each routine does with the fee check, the record and the dates.

Gate. 113 checks where there were 106, all passing.
