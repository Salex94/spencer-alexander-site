# Article topics the audit's evidence supports

Recorded on 23 November 2026. On Spencer's instruction of that day to implement what the audit supports, the eight
topics below are in `scripts/content-backlog.md` as rows 13 to 20, each with its evidence, for the Monday article

## Why these and no others

Two sets of tests on 23 November 2026 pointed at the same gap. On Google, searched as if from Box Hill, seven of the
ten pages on the first page for how much does probate cost Victoria were pages about the cost alone, and the firm
was not among them. On the answer engines, every fee question was answered from pages whose title is the cost
question, published by cost guides and by firms such as Fogarty Oliver Rothschild, Sprintlaw and Campus Lawyers. The
firm's prices were quoted correctly whenever an engine found them, but they sit on pages titled for lawyers in Box
Hill and on the fees page. The article on the cost of contesting a will, published on 23 November 2026, is the one
page the firm has in this form and is the model.

For the two urgent topics, no engine returned the firm for either question. The pages returned were titled as the
question. The site treats a statutory demand from the recipient's side only inside the debt recovery page, the
commercial hub and one FAQ answer, and mentions recovery orders only in passing.

## The topics

Each cost article states the firm's fee by its name, so the fee check ties the figure to the one fee source, gives
the day the price was confirmed, says what the fee covers and assumes, gives the court or government charge from
`charges` in `playbooks/fees.json` with its source and date, and says who pays. Each is tested against the rule on
repeats before it is written, and the existing cost section it grows from then links to it. The practice rotation
still decides which is due.
