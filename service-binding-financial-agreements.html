In brief

Spencer Alexander Lawyers is a Box Hill, Melbourne law firm drafting and advising on binding financial agreements for married and de facto couples, before, during and after a relationship, with the principal overseeing every matter.

  • A binding financial agreement can be made before a marriage or de facto relationship, during it, or after separation or divorce, and can deal with property, financial resources, superannuation and maintenance.
  • It generally binds only if you both sign it, each of you received independent legal advice before signing, each of you was given your lawyer's signed statement of that advice with a copy going to the other, and it has not been ended or set aside.
  • Where a binding agreement covers your property and financial resources, the court's usual power to divide them does not apply.
  • A court can still set an agreement aside on grounds the Act lists, such as fraud, including non-disclosure of a material matter, or unconscionable conduct in making it.
Who we act for

When couples call us about an agreement.

People call before moving in together or marrying, to protect assets built up before the relationship, a business, an expected inheritance or provision for children of an earlier relationship. Others call during a relationship when circumstances change, after separation to make a property settlement binding without going to court, or when a partner's lawyer has sent them an agreement to sign.

How we help

What we do on a financial agreement.

Drafting your agreement

We draft the agreement under the section of the Family Law Act that fits your stage of the relationship, on full financial disclosure, because a court can set aside an agreement obtained by fraud, including non-disclosure of a material matter. The agreement can deal with the home, businesses, trusts, inheritances and superannuation. Any provision for maintenance must name the person and the amount or value provided for them, or it is void, and no agreement stops the court ordering maintenance for someone who, when it took effect, could not support themselves without an income tested pension, allowance or benefit.

Independent advice and the signed statement

Each of you needs independent legal advice before signing, so each of you has your own lawyer. We advise you on how the agreement affects your rights and on its advantages and disadvantages to you at that time, and give you the signed statement of that advice the Act requires, often called a certificate of independent legal advice.

Reviewing an agreement you have been asked to sign

If your partner's lawyer has drafted the agreement, we read it against your circumstances, tell you plainly what you would be giving up, negotiate changes if you want us to, and give the advice and signed statement if you decide to sign. A wedding date is not a legal deadline, so take the time a proper review needs.

Ending or challenging an agreement

An agreement can be ended only by a later financial agreement or a written termination agreement, made with the same independent advice and signed statements. We also advise when an agreement is relied on after separation, act to enforce it or to have it set aside, and appear in court on either side.

Time limits

The dates that matter.

No deadline applies to making an agreement, but leave time before a wedding. A court can set an agreement aside where a party's conduct in making it was unconscionable, or where the agreement is voidable under the general law of contracts, for example for duress or undue influence, and each of those grounds turns on the circumstances in which the agreement was signed.

For a couple who have separated, the time limits for applying to the court for property orders keep running while you negotiate: generally 12 months from the date a divorce order takes effect, or two years from the end of a de facto relationship. After that, an application needs both people's consent or the court's leave, which is not guaranteed. If an agreement is later set aside or found invalid, you generally have at least 12 months from that date to apply. If a wedding is close or a deadline is near, call us on (03) 9125 8355.

What to expect

How a financial agreement is made.

1
Disclosure and terms
You each set out your full financial position and settle the terms you want, and the agreement is drafted from them under the section of the Act that fits your stage of the relationship.
2
Independent advice for each of you
Before signing, each of you receives independent legal advice on how the agreement affects your rights and its advantages and disadvantages, and each lawyer signs a statement that the advice was given.
3
Signing and exchange
You both sign, and a copy of each lawyer's signed statement goes to the other party or their lawyer. If the agreement is made before you separate, its terms on dividing property take effect only once a separation declaration is signed after separation, or on divorce or death.

Good to know

Common questions about financial agreements.

Is a prenup legally binding in Australia?

Yes, if it is made as a binding financial agreement under the Family Law Act and meets the Act's requirements. You must both sign it, each of you must have received independent legal advice before signing and been given your lawyer's signed statement of that advice, with a copy going to the other, and it must not have been ended or set aside. A court can declare an agreement you both signed binding where a requirement about the advice or the statements was missed, but only if it would otherwise be unjust and inequitable.

Can a court set aside a binding financial agreement?

Yes, but only on grounds the Act lists. They include fraud, including non-disclosure of a material matter, and an agreement that is void, voidable or unenforceable, for example for duress or undue influence. A court can also set one aside for unconscionable conduct in making it, for a change of circumstances that makes it impracticable to carry out, for a material change in a child's care, welfare and development that would cause hardship, or where it was made to defraud or defeat a creditor or another partner's claim.

Can de facto couples make a binding financial agreement?

Yes, before, during or after a de facto relationship, under the Act's own provisions for de facto couples and with the same requirement of independent legal advice for each of you. An agreement made as de facto partners stops being binding if you later marry each other, so a couple who may marry should have it drafted with that in mind.

Is a financial agreement better than consent orders?

It depends on where you are. Consent orders are made by the court, which must be satisfied they are just and equitable, while a binding financial agreement is a private contract that no court approves, and it can be made before a relationship even begins. After separation either can make a settlement binding, and we advise which suits your situation.

How much does a binding financial agreement cost?

A binding financial agreement is from $3,025 per party including GST, for an agreement drafted from terms you have agreed. Advice on an agreement drafted by the other party's lawyer is from $1,100 including GST, and our fees page sets out what each covers and what costs more.
